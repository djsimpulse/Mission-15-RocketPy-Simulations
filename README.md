# Mission 15 RocketPy Simulation

Trajectory simulation materials for **DJS Impulse – Mission Garuda**, submitted for the **LASC 2026 1 km Apogee Category**.

## Contents

The ZIP archive contains:

- RocketPy ballistic flight simulation
- RocketPy nominal flight simulation
- Motor thrust curve (`.eng`)
- Drag coefficient data for the Garuda and payload

## Simulation Conditions

- **Apogee Category:** 1 km
- **Atmosphere:** ISA
- **Launch Site Altitude:** 495 m
- **Coordinates:** -21.90795°, -48.96156°
- **Rail Length:** 4 m
- **Azimuth:** 90°
- **Elevation:** 80°
- **Wind:** 10 km/h from East

All required simulation files are provided in `Mission_15_simulations.zip`.
